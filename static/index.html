How a payment moves through PINPOINT

  1. Creator endorsesStates the product and price they stand behind.
  2. Policy is signedEd25519 signature over payee, SKU, ceiling, site and expiry.
  3. Buyer checks outThe checkout proposes an offer.
  4. Offer is checkedServer compares every pinned field with the signature.
  5. PayPal authorizesFunds are held, not captured. Risky orders wait.
  6. Creator decidesA signed approve or void for held orders only.
  7. Outcome is read backCapture or void is confirmed from the gateway, not assumed.

Who decides what

The signature decides

Only the creator-signed policy can authorize a payee, product and price. No model output can widen it.

AI can only hold

A configured model can flag a misleading ad and place an order on hold. It can never capture money or prove a deepfake.

The gateway confirms

Every capture and void is read back from the payment gateway before PINPOINT reports it.

Out of scope: checkouts that don’t call PINPOINT. Evidence about them is for investigation, not enforcement.
