BEHIND THE TRUST

Not a badge.
A bound promise.

A creator can endorse an offer, but a copied video or look-alike storefront shouldn't get to change the deal. PINPOINT checks the signed details where it counts: checkout.

01

THE ENDORSEMENT

They sign the actual terms.

The creator's signed policy records the merchant, product identifier, maximum price, site and expiration. In this demo, browser-held signing keys illustrate the mechanism; identity isn't independently verified.

✍
02

THE CHECKOUT

The details have to match.

When a buyer continues, the server verifies the signature and compares the offer to its pinned terms. A mismatch can stop checkout before a PayPal order is created.

≡
03

THE PAYMENT

Approval isn't the finish line.

In the full backend, authorization and capture are separate. A merchant-side settlement decision rechecks the signed policy and gateway observations. Suspicious content can trigger review, but AI alone doesn't authorize money movement.

ABOUT THIS EXPERIENCE

A working product concept. Not a live store.

This customer-facing website is connected to the same PINPOINT backend as the Security Lab. Listings are generated from currently active demo policy records. It doesn't sell or ship physical products.

Mock payments are simulated, and Sandbox payments use PayPal test accounts. This site doesn't hold an administrator token and cannot perform protected capture or void actions.
